Alert keys

This page makes the key pair that lets your Supabase project send notifications to your devices, plus a secret for the hourly check. Everything is generated here in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is saved when you close the page.

VAPID_PUBLIC_KEYGoes in two places: js/config.js in your repo, and Supabase → Edge Functions → Secrets.
VAPID_PRIVATE_KEYSecret. Supabase → Edge Functions → Secrets only. Never in your repo or in chat.
CRON_SECRETSecret. Supabase → Edge Functions → Secrets, and in the hourly schedule (supabase/alerts.sql).

Keep these somewhere safe, such as your password manager. If you generate new keys later, every device has to turn alerts on again.
